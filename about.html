About this job board

How listings are collected, refreshed, and covered.

Job boardAbout & coverage

Update frequency

The board is configured to refresh automatically once a day via GitHub Actions, plus manual runs. Scheduled runs can be delayed or fail. The latest successful data update is shown on the job board. Job postings may close between updates.

We collect publicly available job listings and use broad business-role keyword filters. These listings are not verified MBA-only positions; confirm eligibility, visa requirements, and application deadlines on the employer's site.
